For our listeners who might not be familiar, what are positional embeddings and why are they so important?

REPORTER: Positional embeddings, Arjav, are a fundamental concept in modern large language models, or LLMs, particularly those based on the Transformer architecture. Essentially, LLMs process text without an inherent understanding of word order – they treat input words as a "bag of words." Positional embeddings are vectors added to the input embeddings of words to inject information about their relative or absolute position within a sequence. This is crucial because the meaning of a sentence often depends heavily on word order. Without positional embeddings, an LLM wouldn't distinguish between "dog bites man" and "man bites dog." They allow the model to understand syntax, grammar, and context that relies on sequence, making LLMs capable of coherent and grammatically correct language generation and comprehension. Understanding them is key to grasping how these powerful models actually work.
