REPORTER: Cloudflare's announcement of an OHTTP gateway is a significant step forward for internet privacy. OHTTP stands for Oblivious HTTP. Essentially, it’s a new privacy-enhancing proxy service that allows users to send HTTP requests to a destination server without that server knowing the user’s true IP address. It works by routing the request through two separate intermediaries: a proxy and a gateway. The proxy knows your IP address but doesn't know your final destination, and the gateway knows your final destination but doesn't know your IP address.
