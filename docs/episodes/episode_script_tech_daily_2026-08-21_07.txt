REPORTER: This is indeed a monumental development for Cassandra, which has traditionally been known for its high availability and scalability at the cost of strict transactional consistency. ACID properties – Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability – are the cornerstone of traditional relational databases, ensuring transactions are processed reliably. Introducing true ACID transactions in Cassandra 6 would represent a paradigm shift. This move would significantly broaden Cassandra's applicability, allowing it to compete in areas traditionally dominated by relational databases, by offering the best of both worlds: strong consistency alongside massive scale and availability.
