Before we go, here's a surprising fun fact from the annals of computing history. While we talk about AI and sophisticated debugging today, the very first "computer bug" wasn't a coding error at all. In 1947, engineers working on the Harvard Mark II Aiken Relay Calculator discovered a malfunction. After some investigation, they found a moth had flown into one of the relays, literally causing a "bug" in the system.
